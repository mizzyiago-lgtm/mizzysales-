How do I add money?

Open Deposit from the dashboard, pick a payment method and follow the steps. Your balance updates after payment is confirmed.

How do I buy a product?

Open the Marketplace, choose a product and tap Buy. Your purchase appears under My Orders.

My number or order has a problem

Open My Orders or Number History to see its status. If it still looks wrong, contact Support with the order details.
